    Biography

    John Reburn is a Land Agent with Peoples Company, assisting landowners, farmers and investors in successfully selling and acquiring farmland. His primary focus is land brokerage, using public auctions, traditional listings and private treaty sales depending on the property and the client’s preference.

    John serves as a resource on the transactions that are rarely simple — estate sales, 1031 tax-deferred exchanges, land contracts, rights of first refusal and partial ownership interests — and works to make each one clear to every party at the table.

    Before joining the brokerage team, John worked as a Land Appraiser, and that valuation background shapes how he prices and positions a farm. He earned a bachelor’s degree in Finance and Real Estate from the University of Northern Iowa and served nine years in the Iowa Army National Guard, including a deployment to Iraq.
