    Renewable Consulting

    Contracts that outlast a generation.

    Solar leases and wind agreements introduce revenue streams that behave fundamentally differently than traditional agricultural income. These contracts extend 20 to 40 years, include complex decommissioning provisions, and tie payments to developer financial stability, project completion risk, and evolving regulatory incentives.

    When renewable opportunities emerge in the area of your land assets, we ensure you understand the contractual provisions that matter:

    • True market value and realistic project timelines
    • Decommissioning bonds
    • CPI escalation clauses
    • Early termination rights
    • Restrictions on future land use

    Partner

    We collaborate with Farm First Energy to evaluate renewable opportunities and negotiate lease terms that protect landowner interests while facilitating economically viable projects. Their platform connects landowners with vetted developers who have demonstrated track records of project completion, appropriate capitalization, and responsible site management.

    Partner

    Logo

    Peoples Company works with Lease Gen for specialized renewable asset valuations and certified appraisals. Their assessments provide defensible figures for estate planning, partnership transitions, and disposition decisions.

    Dispositions

    Put your assets in front of every buyer at once.

    Selling mineral interests or renewable leases requires specialized marketing strategies and valuation expertise. Peoples Company provides comprehensive disposition services that combine thorough asset valuation, strategic marketing execution, and competitive sale processes designed to maximize realized value.

    Our team handles complete pre-sale preparation, so buyers receive the information required for rapid underwriting:

    • Production analysis
    • Title review
    • Lease documentation
    • Financial modeling
    • Targeted campaigns with production mapping and cash flow summaries
