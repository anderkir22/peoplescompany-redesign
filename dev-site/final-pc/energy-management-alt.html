    One Relationship

    One partner for every energy asset on your land.

    Oil and gas royalties, solar and wind leases, pipeline easements: energy income rarely comes from a single source, and each one brings its own contracts, operators, and paperwork. Peoples Company brings them together under one relationship, pairing our land expertise with specialist partners so every interest is tracked, protected, and positioned to perform.

    Mineral & Royalty Management

    Every well, every operator, every payment accounted for.

    Mineral and royalty interests require ongoing oversight to ensure proper revenue collection, lease negotiation and compliance, and protection of ownership rights, across multiple wells, operators, and jurisdictions.

    What we handle

    • Division order reviews
    • Ongoing lease monitoring and negotiation
    • Direct coordination with operators
    • Document processing and lease compliance tracking
    • Transparent reporting on asset performance and revenue

    Without detailed management, owners risk underpayment, missed lease renewals, or unexercised rights through administrative oversight.

    Disposition & Valuation

    Put your assets in front of every buyer at once.

    Energy asset buyers, from institutional funds to regional operators, each employ distinct underwriting processes and return requirements. Our disposition services combine thorough valuation, strategic marketing, and competitive sale processes designed to maximize realized value.

    Sequential negotiation

    One conversation after another

    Each offer is negotiated on its own, and the next buyer only sees the asset if the last one walks away.

    Structured auction

    The entire buyer universe, simultaneously

    Competitive tension across institutional, private equity, and individual operator buyers consistently produces superior pricing.

    Structured auctions with Mid-Continent Energy Exchange

    This Wichita-based firm specializes in energy asset auctions and provides the platform to reach qualified buyers. Our team handles complete pre-sale preparation, so buyers receive what they need for rapid underwriting.

    1. Production analysis

      Production history and cash flow summaries that make value clear.

    2. Title review

      Ownership confirmed before the asset goes to market.

    3. Lease documentation

      Leases and records assembled for buyer review.

    4. Financial modeling

      Data-driven presentation, marketed through industry platforms and curated buyer networks.

    Renewable Energy Assets

    Contracts that outlast a generation.

    20–40year terms on solar
    and wind agreements

    Solar leases and wind agreements behave fundamentally differently than agricultural income, with complex decommissioning provisions and payments tied to developer financial stability, project completion risk, and evolving regulatory incentives.

    Lease terms that protect landowner interests and projects that get built.

    Farm First Energy connects landowners with vetted developers who have demonstrated track records of project completion, appropriate capitalization, and responsible site management, plus market intelligence on lease rates, developer reputations, and regional project activity.

    The provisions that matter

    • Decommissioning bonds
    • CPI escalation clauses
    • Early termination rights
    • Restrictions on future land use
    • True market value and realistic project timelines
    Lease Gen

    What a renewable lease is actually worth today.

    Lease Gen brings decades of renewable energy experience to specialized valuations and certified appraisals, with frameworks that account for project age, developer track record, regional cap rates, and market-specific factors that generic appraisal methods miss.

    Defensible figures for

    • Estate planning
    • Partnership transitions
    • Disposition decisions
    • Weighing completion risk, developer creditworthiness, and regional capacity
