    Services

    Peoples Company offers a full suite of land-related services under one roof, including brokerage, appraisal, farm management, insurance, and capital markets. Our integrated team of specialists works together to deliver comprehensive solutions, giving clients deep expertise and local market knowledge in every transaction.

    Over the past 50 years, Peoples Company has grown into a U.S. market leader in agricultural land transactions and management services, including brokerage, appraisal, and land management as well as a complete suite of land investment advisory services. Leveraging our national scale and local expertise, we serve landowners, operators, investors, and corporate clients with large, geographically diverse portfolios. Our integrated services give you a single point of contact for all your land needs, whether you are buying, selling, managing, valuing, financing, or protecting land.

    Land Brokerage & Auctions

    Farmland listings and competitive auction services with a 40,000+ buyer database and national reach. Our brokers pair local market knowledge with a national platform to market each property to a vast buyer pool, including large farm operators, individuals utilizing 1031 exchanges, institutional investors, family offices, and high-net-worth individuals.

    Explore Land Brokerage & Auctions →

    Land Management

    Full-service stewardship, operator oversight, lease negotiation, and financial reporting. Peoples Company farm managers work closely with top operators to actively improve soil health, leverage production data, coordinate capital improvements, and optimize operational efficiency, benefiting both the operator who farms the ground and the investor who owns it.

    Explore Land Management →

    Agricultural Appraisal

    Certified, data-driven land valuation supported by proprietary comparable-sales modeling. Our appraisers bring local market knowledge and national data together to deliver defensible values for acquisitions, estate planning, partnership transitions, and disposition decisions, whether the asset is a single farm or a multi-state portfolio.

    Explore Agricultural Appraisal →

    Capital Markets

    Separately managed farmland portfolios for institutional investors, family offices, and high-net-worth individuals, backed by the most comprehensive farmland services platform in the country. Our team delivers an integrated approach to farmland investment, from due diligence and acquisition through asset management, customized reporting, and disposition.

    Explore Capital Markets →

    Energy Management

    Energy assets can represent substantial return generators for landowners, and Peoples Company brings the specialized expertise required to manage, acquire, or sell them. We coordinate directly with operators, track lease compliance, and verify every payment, with oversight of mineral interests, renewable leases, and dispositions in one place.

    Explore Energy Management →

    Crop Insurance

    Full-line risk management solutions including crop insurance to optimize revenue and mitigate uncertainty across all business lines. We integrate crop insurance into every decision we make, and our partnership with Strategic Farm Marketing gives clients comprehensive analysis, clear recommendations, and broad product access through one relationship.

    Explore Crop Insurance →

    Corporate Services

    Full-service, tailored solutions for corporate clients with large, geographically diverse portfolios. Leveraging our national scale and local expertise, the strategy and experience of our internal team, coupled with the knowledge and contacts of our expert consultants, allow us to execute on complex portfolios, producing results that may not otherwise be realized.

    Explore Corporate Services →

    Tell us about your land and what you are looking to accomplish, and we will connect you with the Peoples Company professional best suited to help. Whether you are buying, selling, managing, valuing, financing, or protecting land, one conversation is all it takes to get started.
