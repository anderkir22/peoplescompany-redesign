    Farm Operators

    Building partnerships with exceptional farm operators is fundamental to how Peoples Company manages farmland assets. Our team believes the most successful business outcomes are built on relationships where landowner objectives and operator expertise align to drive measurable improvements.

    Farmland ownership generates value through two forms of return: annual income and long-term asset appreciation. Our management approach is designed to enhance both. Peoples Company farm managers work closely with top operators to actively improve soil health, leverage production data, coordinate capital improvements, and optimize operational efficiency. These investments create compounding benefits: improved yields strengthen annual returns while enhanced land quality drives appreciation, benefiting both the operator who farms the ground and the investor who owns it.

    We view operator relationships as long-term commitments, not annual transactions. When we identify talented producers who share our values, we foster these relationships, prioritize their growth, and offer them access to opportunities to strategically grow their operations. We also recognize these valuable partners by hosting regional operator receptions and honoring a “Farmer of the Year” at the Land Investment Expo each January.

    The Operators We Partner With

    Peoples Company’s success is directly tied to partnering with operators who approach their work with the same professionalism and long-term perspective we bring to land management. We seek partners who value open communication, embrace conservation practices, and understand that sustainable returns require sustainable stewardship.

    Transparency & Communication

    Effective land management requires clear information flow between all stakeholders. Our managers build collaborative relationships with operators that ensure consistent landowner updates and well-defined expectations for both management and production goals.

    Sustainable Practices

    Our portfolio is managed to the Leading Harvest Sustainability Standard, the industry’s leading farmland stewardship framework. We actively partner with operators who view conservation as sound business practice and implement sustainable practices within their operations.

    Long-Term Approach

    Our managers work with operators to implement improvement plans that drive future production improvement, and therefore value. This approach extends to our leasing relationships; we minimize tenant turnover, align incentives long-term outcomes, and maximize performance.

    Operator Appreciation Dinners

    Recognizing the dedication of our farm operators is more than courtesy, it’s strategic relationship management. Each year, we host regional appreciation dinners that bring together the tenants who execute our management vision across the country for our landowners. These gatherings demonstrate our commitment to the people who translate strategy into production and are a great opportunity to connect and gain market and operational insights with farmland stakeholders.

    Farmer of the Year

    In 2023, Peoples Company created the “Farmer of the Year” to recognize excellence within our operator network. This distinction honors an operator who exemplifies a commitment to production, sustainability, stewardship, and thorough communication. Recipients are honored at the Land Investment Expo each January, where their dedication to the land is showcased as a model for the industry.

    Kevin Babb is the winner of the 2025 Peoples Company Farmer of the Year. From his fourth-generation roots in Champaign County to leading a high-performing corn and soybean operation, Kevin shows how “Cadillac” agronomy (balanced fertility, premium inputs, and precise timing) translates into stronger yields, healthier soil, and long-term landowner trust.

    Operator Database

Operators interested in adding acres to their operation within our managed portfolio are encouraged to connect with a Peoples Company Farm Manager in their area. This process allows our managers to evaluate operators before opportunities come available, ensuring efficient transitions and planning for both landowners and operators.

    Structure a Sale-Leaseback

    Secure access to productive acres is vital to the profitability of many farm operators. Sale-leaseback transactions are an excellent tool for operators needing flexibility and predictability, allowing operators to monetize owned real estate while maintaining farming rights through long-term lease agreements.

    This structure provides solutions for common agricultural scenarios. A sale-leaseback creates an opportunity for an investor to acquire the asset while establishing a long-term relationship with an operator, with the opportunity for the operator to buy back or acquire an asset at the end of the lease term.

    Peoples Company Capital Markets connects these transactions to our network of farmland investors (high-net-worth individuals, family offices, institutional funds, and 1031 exchange buyers) who seek quality agricultural assets with experienced operators. The arrangement is mutually beneficial because operators gain capital and production capacity, and investors acquire improved, income-producing farmland with an established, capable operator.

    Gain Access to Capital

    Growth in agriculture increasingly requires access to flexible capital structures beyond conventional bank financing. Peoples Company Capital Markets designs tailored solutions for operators seeking to expand production footprints while maintaining balance sheet discipline.

    Peoples Company Capital Markets structures transactions that provide working capital, facilitate strategic acquisitions, or restructure existing debt, all while preserving operator control and decision-making authority. These structures can be calibrated to various timelines, from bridge capital for a single season, to multi-year arrangements that support systematic expansion.
