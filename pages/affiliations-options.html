Professional Affiliations: layout options

Four ways to lay out the section with the ASFMRA and Appraisal Institute logos separated from the photo and sitting directly on white. The dashed boxes are stand-ins: the current photo has the two logos baked into it, so each organization needs its own logo file. Photos here are placeholders too.

Option A

Two equal columns split by a hairline, each led by its logo. No photo. The cleanest and most symmetrical of the four.

Credentials

Professional Affiliations

Peoples Company appraisers are proud members of industry-leading organizations that uphold the highest standards in ethics, education, and expertise.

ASFMRA logo
American Society of Farm Managers and Rural Appraisers (ASFMRA)

Our team includes Accredited Rural Appraisers (ARA) and members actively involved in ASFMRA, an organization dedicated to advancing the appraisal profession in agriculture and rural property.

Appraisal Institute logo
Appraisal Institute (AI)

Several of our appraisers are members of the Appraisal Institute, a global association known for rigorous professional standards and advanced valuation credentials such as the MAI and AI-GRS.
