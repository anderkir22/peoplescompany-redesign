PC White | #FFFFFF

PC Black = headings, nav, primary ink, and all body copy (large and small). PC Navy = fills, buttons, eyebrows, borders, and links on light — never body copy. PC Medium Blue = nav-link hover, "What We Do" eyebrow, light-background buttons. PC Blue = bright accent, used on dark backgrounds only (it fails contrast on white). PC Light Blue = hairline borders, pale section tint, and secondary text on dark. PC Soft White = subtle card / section fill. No red, no green anywhere in the palette. When a color is named without a hex, it means the PC version (e.g. "black" = PC Black #07162C, not #000000).
