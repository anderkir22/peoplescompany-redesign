    Mineral & Royalty Management

    How every payment gets checked.

    Without detailed management, owners risk underpayment, missed lease renewals, or unexercised rights through administrative oversight. Scroll through how we close those gaps.

    1. 01

      Many wells, many operators

      Managing mineral holdings means tracking production across multiple wells and operators, with regulatory obligations that vary by jurisdiction.

    2. 02

      Every payment verified

      Valor’s mineral.tech® system, purpose-built for energy administration, delivers detailed production tracking and automated revenue reconciliation, without paying two separate service providers.

    3. 03

      Leases watched

      Division order reviews, lease compliance tracking, and ongoing negotiation. We coordinate directly with operators so renewals are not missed.

    4. 04

      One clear report

      Transparent reporting that gives you complete visibility into asset performance and revenue streams, quarterly or monthly, at the level of involvement you choose.
