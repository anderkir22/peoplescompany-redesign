Service pages · Review

Video Experience Options

Every service page will carry a video, so the treatment we pick here becomes the pattern for all seven. Each option below is live and plays the same Energy Management video. Each one starts from what is on the page today: the Vimeo thumbnail with the pulsing play button. Starting one option stops any other that is playing.

Every option plays with the PC control bar in place of Vimeo's: the PC Diamond is the playhead, small diamonds on the track mark the chapters, and hovering the track shows the time and chapter name. The bar fades out while the video plays and returns when you move the pointer. Requires Vimeo Paid Plan.

The text beside the video is optional on every service page. Use the switch in the lower left corner to see every option with the text or with the video on its own.

Options can be combined. One way to open the video (1 to 3) can pair with the hover preview (4) and any of the extras (5 to 7).

Option 1 · How it opens

Theater Mode

Play grows the poster out of its frame into a large stage while the page dims to PC Black. Esc, the close button or a click outside returns you to the page exactly where you were.

  • WhyThe video gets the whole screen without leaving the page. It feels like an event, not a widget.
  • Trade-offThe page is hidden while it plays, so the copy and form beside it are out of sight until the viewer closes it.

Option 2 · How it opens

Expand in Place

The copy column fades away and the video widens to fill the full band. A slim bar under the player names the video and offers Collapse, which stops the video and brings the copy back.

  • WhyA bigger picture without a modal. The viewer never feels they have left the page, and the rest of the page stays scrollable.
  • Trade-offSmaller than theater mode, and the band grows taller as it widens, which shifts the content below.

Option 3 · How it opens

Lightbox with Context

Play opens a dialog with the player on the left and a rail on the right: title, runtime, clickable chapters, and a way to start the conversation without closing the video first.

  • WhyThe call to action is beside the video the whole time. Chapters let owners jump straight to the part about their asset.
  • Trade-offThe video plays smaller than in theater mode, and the rail needs chapter data for every video.

    Option 4 · Poster

    Silent Preview on Hover

    Hover the poster and the video quietly plays a muted eight-second loop under the play button. On a phone the loop starts when the poster scrolls into view. A click plays the full video, with sound, from the start.

    • WhyMotion catches the eye without autoplay sound or a heavy video in the hero. Nothing loads until the viewer shows interest.
    • Developer noteHere the loop is a segment of the real video (0:20 to 0:28) in Vimeo background mode (Requires Vimeo Paid Plan). In production, an exported 6 to 8 second muted MP4 loads faster. Turned off for reduced motion.

    Chapter List

    The copy column gives way to a list of chapters. A click on any chapter starts the video at that point, and the chapter playing now is highlighted with a thin progress line as it plays.

    • WhyA royalty owner can skip to royalties. It also shows at a glance how much the video covers.

      Option 6 · More than a video

      Ending Card

      When the video finishes, a PC Black panel replaces Vimeo's end screen with the next step: start the conversation, watch again, or move on to the next service.

      • WhyThe end of the video is the moment the viewer is most interested. Without this they get related videos from other Vimeo accounts.
      • CMSNothing new: the button points at the page's own form, and "up next" is the next service in the menu.

      Option 7 · More than a video

      Sticky Mini-Player

      Start the video, then scroll on. When the player leaves the screen it shrinks into the lower right corner and keeps playing. Scroll back up and it settles into place, or close it from the corner.

      • WhyThe viewer can keep listening while they read the services and partners below, so the video works with the page instead of against it.
      • Trade-offOn a phone the corner player covers content. I would turn it off below 768.
