    Farmland as an Asset Class

    Farmland is among the most stable and resilient asset classes available to investors, offering consistent income, long-term appreciation, and a hedge against inflation that acts independently of traditional markets. With rising global food demand and a fundamentally constrained land supply, the structural case for farmland as a long-term investment has never been stronger. Peoples Company Capital Markets connects capital with distinctive farmland opportunities across the country’s most productive agricultural regions, delivering outcomes built around capital preservation, portfolio diversification, and sustainable long-term value creation.

    Our Capital Markets team publishes the annual National Land Values report and a growing library of farmland market research, establishing Peoples Company as one of the leading voices in farmland market intelligence.

    Explore our industry research to learn more about the farmland asset class.

    Tailored Solutions

    Capital Markets provides comprehensive separate account asset acquisition, management, and advisory services for investors who want the benefits of farmland but need professional acquisition and management services, from first-time farmland allocators to institutional investors managing large, diversified portfolios. Our team implements investment strategies by sourcing, acquiring, and managing investable farmland assets on behalf of the client. The separate account structure provides investors with direct ownership, where they control which assets to acquire, how they are managed, and when to divest them with the expert advice and assistance of the Capital Markets team throughout the life of the investment.

    Farmland Investing – Our Service

    Our approach to farmland investment is grounded in acquiring high-quality assets in strong agricultural production regions where professional operators, productive soils, and long-term appreciation create distinct opportunities. By adhering to disciplined acquisition criteria and a focus on long-term performance, our team builds portfolios designed to deliver consistent income and durable appreciation across market cycles.

    Portfolio Creation

    When building a portfolio, we focus on acquiring high-quality assets in thriving agricultural markets with professional farm operators. We prioritize establishing a strong foundation of high-quality row crop assets with straightforward operating structures before expanding into value-add and permanent crop opportunities. Our team works closely with clients to develop and refine acquisition mandates that reflect their objectives, acquisition mandates, and target markets.

    Deal Sourcing, Due Diligence, and Acquisition

    We identify and evaluate farmland opportunities aligned with client mandates, presenting assets that meet acquisition guidelines. Once client interest is confirmed, we manage the acquisition process from letter of intent and purchase negotiations to closing while conducting rigorous due diligence, including historical performance review, comparable sales analysis, third-party reports, and financial modeling.

    Asset Management and Portfolio Reporting

    Capital Markets’ asset management focuses on sustainability and value-add opportunities to optimize annual cash yield and maximize long-term appreciation. Our team coordinates management decisions with Peoples Company’s farm management division, which provides the local expertise and operational depth that active asset management demands. Investor communication is a priority: our team provides quarterly financial reporting, mid-year performance reviews, and annual portfolio-level reporting, supplemented by a quarterly newsletter keeping limited partners informed of portfolio developments and market conditions.

    Our Process To Support Your Portfolio

    With over $4 billion in farmland under management across more than 25 states, Peoples Company brings national market intelligence paired with deep local sourcing expertise across every major agricultural region. Our proprietary tools, including FarmWorth™ and our real-time asset database, enable our team to identify high-quality opportunities, stress-test assumptions, and provide clients with optionality at every stage of the investment.
