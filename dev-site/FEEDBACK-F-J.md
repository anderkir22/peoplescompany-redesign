# Version F–J Variation Feedback

Ten decision points comparing homepage versions **F, G, H, I, J**. Each records the options in play and the feedback received.

> **DECISION — President & VP (2026-07-27): use Version J as the base for the FINAL version.**
> Built as **Version K** (`version-k.html`). Their per-item picks are recorded as **President & VP**
> below; the full list of Version J → K adjustments is in the **"Version J → K adjustments"** section
> at the end.

---

## 1. Overall color palette
- **Black + blue** — F / I
- **White + PC blue** — G
- **White + green** — H / J

**Feedback:**
- **Lay:** White + green (H / J)
- **Kiron:** Black + blue (F / I)
- **Erin:** F or G (equal)
- **President & VP:** White + green (J) — the chosen base. Moving toward a **deeper green/teal**;
  **dislike the mint-green** accents. Colors to be worked separately (Erin to bring a few color-scheme
  options); do not fully mock up palettes in Claude.

**Overall summary:** No clean consensus (Lay + P&VP = green; Kiron + Erin lean blue). **Decided anyway:**
the **President & VP call governs → White + deep green/teal** is the base, with mint dropped. Final palette
is a separate track (Erin's color options), so this stays locked to the *family* (deep green/teal), not the
exact shades.
