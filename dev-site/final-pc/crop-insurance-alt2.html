    How the floor works.

    The USDA’s Federal Crop Insurance program makes U.S. farmland a uniquely resilient asset class. Scroll through ten illustrative years.

      A good run

      Most seasons, revenue sits comfortably above where it needs to be.

    2. 02

      Then the year turns

      Weather challenges or market volatility can pull a single season far below the rest, for the farmer, the landowner, and the investor alike.

    3. 03

      The floor holds

      By guaranteeing minimum annual revenue, the Federal Crop Insurance program covers the shortfall, reducing operational risk for farmers.

    4. 04

      Year after year

      Protecting asset value for landowners and investors. Established 1938, the program is backed by ongoing research and actuarial development.

    Family Farmers

    Why It Matters

    Crop insurance runs through every service.

    From evaluating insurable yields during acquisition to structuring lease agreements, it shapes the work across the platform.

    Guaranteed minimum revenue protects operational performance and stabilizes cash flow, regardless of weather challenges or market volatility.

    Appraisal & Valuation

    Historical APH data and insurable revenue provide essential benchmarks for asset underwriting and investment analysis.

    Capital Markets

    Investors require quantifiable risk mitigation. Crop insurance delivers measurable downside protection for agricultural portfolios.
