    Overview

    Seeking a Land Agent licensed in the state of Nebraska. The Land Agent is responsible for managing the full brokerage lifecycle — from business development, property research and proposal development through marketing execution, transaction coordination and client communication.

    The role collaborates with internal teams, leverages the Peoples Company platform, implements brokerage processes, manages listings digitally, coordinates closings and ensures contract compliance. Business development carries its own weight here: networking, a professional social media presence and participation in industry events.

    Responsibilities

    Brokerage Proposals & Marketing

    • Research properties and assemble brokerage proposals per client direction
    • Create maps, gather information and write marketing descriptions
    • Collaborate with the marketing team on print and digital materials
    • Install, maintain and remove brokerage signs
    • Manage MLS listings, company website listings and third-party platforms

    Brokerage Transactions

    • Maintain the sales pipeline via the Peoples Company platform
    • Prepare marketing activity and expense reports
    • Follow internal compliance processes
    • Communicate key dates to all parties
    • Coordinate closing procedures, including title review and document preparation

    Client Communication

    • Maintain confidentiality of company records and client information
    • Deliver exceptional customer service through professional communication
    • Share market conditions that support client decision-making

    Technology Utilization

    • Use FarmWorth for mapping and land data analysis
    • Capture property media using drone technology
    • Maintain records in Salesforce, Dropbox and Dotloop

    Business Development & Industry Engagement

    • Maintain a professional social media presence weekly
    • Market services to attorneys, CPAs and financial institutions
    • Cultivate relationships with new clients and referral sources
    • Participate in industry events and trade shows
    • Promote the Land Investment Expo through networking and social media

    Cross-Selling & Collaboration

    • Communicate with the appraisal, management and capital markets teams
    • Share market intelligence on land values and transaction volume
    • Promote the full suite of company services
    • Identify co-listing opportunities
