The rail standard

Four pages carry a side rail, and each was built on its own. This page puts them next to each other, then breaks the difference into eight decisions. Every specimen is drawn with the real values from the page it names, so whatever you pick is already running somewhere.

Work top to bottom and name a winner per decision. Where a page needs to depart from the standard, that becomes a stated addendum rather than a drift.

2. The decisions

All eight are answered. The winner in each row carries a navy ring; the options that lost are kept visible so the record of what was considered survives.

Decision 1 · Corner radius Decided · 12px

12px or 16px, on every rail box. 12px won and is applied.

Decision 2 · Padding inside the box Decided · 24 / 24 / 32

Even on all sides, or heavier at the foot. 24 / 24 / 32 won and is applied - the asymmetric version stops the last row sitting hard against the bottom edge.

Decision 3 · The block heading Decided

The biggest visual difference between the rails. Four treatments are in the build today.

Decision 4 · Item title inside a box Decided

The name of a post, an office, a job, a person.

Decision 5 · Body copy size in a rail Decided

A sentence or two of real prose, sitting in a rail box.

Decision 6 · Hover behaviour Decided

Hover over both. One moves, one does not.

Decision 7 · Rail width and the gap between boxes Decided · 380px, 24px gap

380px and a 24px gap, applied.

Decision 8 · Fills a rail box may take Decided

Three grounds are in use. The question is which are allowed, and what each one means when a reader sees it.

3. The addendums to name

Three devices exist on some rails and not others. Each is either part of the standard, allowed as a named exception, or dropped.

The region pill

10px uppercase on PC Light Blue, the smallest type on the site. Contact's office cards only.

Midwest

The whole-card link

The heading's link is stretched over the whole card, so the card is one big target and the phone number inside stays separately clickable. Contact's office cards and the team cards. Everywhere else, a rail row is an ordinary link.

The PC Blue cap rule

A 3px PC Blue border along the top of each card, repeating the tab underline. Team member rail only, which you have kept as is.
